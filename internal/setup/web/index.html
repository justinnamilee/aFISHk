Set up your fishing companion

This page runs only on this computer. Have WoW open, with FishFry installed and manual cast/reel bound to your fishing key.

1. Identify your game

Click below, then switch to WoW within five seconds. Keep it focused until the countdown finishes.

No game selected

2. Connect your phone

The web server listens on this desktop IP. Use 0.0.0.0 to listen on all IPv4 interfaces.

Enter a real, numeric IPv4 address for your desktop that can be reached over LAN or VPN. Audio cannot use 0.0.0.0 or a hostname, this traffic cannot be proxied.

Audio uses UDP 49160–49180 by default. To change this range, edit udp_min and udp_max in config.json and restart aFISHk. Allow the chosen web and audio ports through your LAN/VPN firewall.

3. Game bindings

Type a binding or select Record and press the matching in-game key. Ctrl and Shift can be combined. Tab leaves recording.
