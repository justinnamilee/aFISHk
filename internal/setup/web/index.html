Click below, then switch to WoW within five seconds. Keep it focused until the countdown finishes.

2. Connect your phone

Use an address reachable through your LAN/VPN. You manage the VPN, DNS and firewall. Default audio ports: UDP 49160–49180; web: TCP 8787.

3. Game bindings

Type a binding or select Record and press the matching in-game key. Ctrl and Shift can be combined. Tab leaves recording.
